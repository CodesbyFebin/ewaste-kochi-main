What is IT Asset Disposition (ITAD)?

IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) is the secure, environmentally responsible process of retiring and disposing of IT equipment such as laptops, desktops, servers, storage devices, and networking gear. We help you protect your data, ensure compliance, and recover maximum value from your end-of-life IT assets.
