What Determines Your E-Waste's Scrap Value?

The scrap value of electronic waste is not arbitrary — it is driven by a specific set of factors that determine how much recoverable material an item contains and how efficiently that material can be extracted. The primary driver is material composition: circuit boards are rich in gold (present in connector pins and component leads), copper (in PCB traces and wiring), and silver (in solder). Working condition dramatically affects pricing because functional devices have refurbishment market value far above their raw material content. Brand and model matter because premium brands use higher-grade materials and retain refurbishment demand. Bulk quantity unlocks better per-unit rates because it reduces our per-unit collection cost. Current commodity prices on global markets (particularly the London Metal Exchange prices for copper, gold and aluminium) directly affect what we can pay. Finally, refurbishability — the ability to resell a device in working condition — is the single biggest value multiplier for any individual item.

⚗️
Material Value
Gold, copper, silver and aluminium content in PCBs, connectors and chassis. Gold-rich server motherboards and circuit boards command the highest material value per kilogram.
✅
Working Condition
Working devices are worth 3–5× more than dead units. A functional laptop that boots and displays correctly may be refurbished and sold — that refurbishment value adds directly to your payment.
🏷️
Brand & Model
Apple, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Cisco, IBM — premium brands retain higher resale value and use better-quality materials. A MacBook typically pays 2–3× more than a no-brand laptop of similar age.
📦
Bulk Quantity
50+ units of any item qualifies for bulk pricing — higher per-unit rates above our standard list. Corporate collections of 500+ units receive custom quotations. The more you have, the better we can pay.
📈
Current Market
Copper and aluminium prices on the London Metal Exchange directly affect scrap rates. We update our prices monthly. When commodity prices rise, your scrap value rises with them — WhatsApp for the latest rates.
🔄
Refurbishability
Can the device be cleaned, repaired and resold? Refurbishable devices command the highest prices. Even partially working items — a laptop with a cracked screen but good motherboard — can have above-average refurbishment potential.
