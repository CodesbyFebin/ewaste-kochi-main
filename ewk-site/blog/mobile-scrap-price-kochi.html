Data Security Before Scrapping Your Mobile in Kochi

Mobile phones contain an enormous amount of sensitive personal data — contacts, messages, banking apps, saved passwords, photos, authentication apps, and business communications. Before selling your phone for scrap in Kochi, ensuring complete data destruction is absolutely critical.

  1. Log out of all accounts — Google/Apple ID, banking apps, WhatsApp, social media.
  2. Remove SIM card and memory card.
  3. Perform factory reset: Android — Settings → General Management → Factory Reset. iPhone — Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content.
  4. For iPhones, disable Activation Lock by signing out of iCloud before handing over.
  5. For Android, encrypt the device before factory reset for maximum security (Settings → Security → Encrypt Device).

Ewaste Kochi provides an additional layer of certified data destruction for all mobile phones collected in Kochi. Even after factory reset, we use specialized software tools to perform NIST-compliant data wiping on mobile storage, ensuring no residual data can be recovered. A data destruction certificate is provided within 24 hours for business phones.

⚠ Note: Never sell your mobile phone to an informal scrap dealer in Kochi without performing a factory reset and disabling Find My/Activation Lock. Data theft from improperly disposed phones is a major risk in Kerala.

What Affects Mobile Scrap Price in Kochi?

Several interconnected factors determine the scrap or buyback price for your mobile phone in Kochi. Understanding these helps you present your device optimally for maximum payout.

  • Model and Brand: iPhone and premium Samsung models command the highest prices. Budget brands fetch lower but still positive scrap values.
  • Age: Phones within 3 years of release command significantly higher prices than older models. New releases typically depreciate 20–30% within the first year.
  • Working Condition: A phone that powers on, makes calls, and connects to Wi-Fi is worth 50–100% more than a non-functioning equivalent.
  • Screen Condition: Cracked screens reduce value by 20–40%. AMOLED screens with no dead pixels command premium prices.
  • Battery Health: Original battery above 80% health adds meaningful value. Replaced non-original batteries reduce price slightly.
  • Storage Size: Higher storage variants (256GB, 512GB, 1TB) consistently command better prices in Kochi.
  • Accessories: Original box, charger, earphones, and unused screen protectors add ₹300–₹1,500 to the price.
  • iCloud/Google Lock Status: A phone locked to a previous owner's account has drastically reduced value — ensure you unlock before selling.
