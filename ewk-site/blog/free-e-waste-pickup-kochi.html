Why Is Our E-Waste Pickup Free in Kochi?

Many people wonder why Ewaste Kochi can offer free e-waste pickup when disposing of old electronics seems like it would be costly. The answer lies in the economic model of certified e-waste recycling.

Electronic waste — even old, broken devices — contains valuable materials: copper wiring, aluminum casings, steel frames, precious metal traces on circuit boards (gold, silver, palladium), rare earth elements in hard drives, and recoverable plastics. When processed correctly at authorized recycling facilities, these materials have real monetary value that covers the cost of collection and processing.

Additionally, for devices with significant resale or refurbishment value (recent laptops, smartphones, servers), the market price we pay for these devices is offset by the value we recover through refurbishment and resale. This creates a sustainable model where free pickup for you is possible because the recycled materials generate sufficient revenue.

Ewaste Kochi's free pickup is also part of our mission: making responsible e-waste disposal accessible to everyone in Kochi regardless of quantity, location, or financial means.
