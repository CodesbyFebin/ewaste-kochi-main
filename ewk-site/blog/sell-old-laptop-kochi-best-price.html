What Affects Laptop Scrap Price?

The same model laptop can fetch very different prices depending on these factors:

  • Processor generation: Intel 10th/11th/12th gen commands much higher prices than 5th/6th gen
  • RAM: 16GB+ RAM boosts resale value significantly over 4GB
  • Storage: SSD-equipped laptops worth more than HDD versions
  • Screen condition: Cracked screens reduce value by 40–60%
  • Brand: Apple, Dell, Lenovo ThinkPad fetch premium over generic brands
  • Battery health: Working battery improves resale; dead battery doesn't greatly affect scrap price
  • Charger included: Missing charger can reduce offer by ₹500–₹2,000

How to Safely Prepare Your Laptop Before Selling

Before handing over any device, protect yourself:

  1. Back up your data — copy everything to an external drive or cloud storage
  2. Sign out of all accounts — Google, iCloud, Microsoft, banking apps
  3. Disable Find My (Apple) — otherwise the buyer can't use the device
  4. Factory reset the device — Windows: Settings → Recovery → Reset. Mac: Erase All Content and Settings
  5. For sensitive data, request certified wipe — a factory reset is not sufficient for highly sensitive information
