Factors That Determine Laptop Scrap Value in Kochi

Understanding what drives laptop scrap value in Kochi helps you present your device optimally and time your sale for maximum return. Several key variables interact to determine the final price you receive.

  • Model Year & Age: The single biggest driver — post-2021 laptops command 3–5× the price of equivalent 2016–2018 models in Kochi.
  • Processor Generation: 11th/12th/13th gen Intel Core, AMD Ryzen 5000/7000, and Apple M-series chips attract the best prices. Older Core 2 Duo and early i3/i5 models have minimal market value.
  • RAM: 16GB and 32GB configurations add ₹2,000–₹5,000 to Kochi scrap prices. 4GB RAM machines are worth significantly less.
  • Storage: SSD-equipped laptops are worth considerably more than equivalent HDD models. NVMe SSDs command the highest premium.
  • Working Condition: A fully functional laptop commands 50–80% more than a non-booting equivalent of the same model.
  • Physical Condition: Cracked screens, broken hinges, damaged keyboard, or missing keys reduce price by 20–40%.
  • Battery Health: Original battery at 70%+ health adds value. Swollen or dead batteries reduce pricing.
  • Accessories: Original charger, box, and warranty documentation add ₹500–₹2,500 to the final price.
