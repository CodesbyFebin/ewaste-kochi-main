Why Recycling E-Waste Matters

Electronics contain both hazardous materials and valuable metals. Proper recycling prevents contamination and recovers resources.

India generates over 3.2 million tonnes of e-waste annually. Kerala's E-Waste Rules 2022 require businesses and consumers to responsibly dispose of all electronic waste through CPCB-authorized recyclers like Ewaste Kochi.
