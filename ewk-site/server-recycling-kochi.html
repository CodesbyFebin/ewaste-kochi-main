Guaranteed Data Security & DPDP Compliance

When decommissioning servers, data security is our absolute highest priority. We eliminate the risk of data leaks by strictly adhering to international standards:

  • NIST 800-88 Guidelines: We utilise certified multi-pass software wiping (like Blancco) for drives that retain value, completely overwriting all sectors so data is forensically unrecoverable.
  • DoD 5220.22-M Standards: Military-grade data sanitisation.
  • Physical Hard Drive Shredding: For end-of-life drives or highly classified data, our industrial shredders physically destroy the drives down to small metallic fragments. (On-site shredding available upon request).
  • Certificate of Destruction: For every server and hard drive processed, we provide a serialized, auditable Certificate of Data Destruction, protecting you against DPDP Act liability.

The Server Decommissioning Process

Our turnkey ITAD process minimizes disruption to your IT team:

  1. Site Audit & Valuation: Our engineers visit your data center to inventory the assets and provide a transparent financial quote.
  2. Secure De-installation: We safely un-rack servers, switches, and UPS systems, ensuring no damage to surrounding live infrastructure.
  3. Chain of Custody Transport: Assets are securely loaded into GPS-tracked vehicles and transported directly to our certified facility.
  4. Data Destruction & Processing: Data is permanently wiped or drives are shredded. Usable components are harvested for remarketing, while the rest is sustainably recycled for raw material recovery.
  5. Compliance Reporting: You receive a comprehensive ITAD report, Green Certificate, and payment for the asset value.
