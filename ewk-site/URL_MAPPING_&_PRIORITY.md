## 🎯 URL SLUG BEST PRACTICES

**DO:**
- ✅ Hyphen-separated: `/free-e-waste-pickup-kochi/`
- ✅ Include primary keyword near front
- ✅ Add location at end: `-kochi` or `-kerala`
- ✅ Keep it short: max 5 words
- ✅ Match user search intent exactly

**DON'T:**
- ❌ No underscores: `/free_e_waste_pickup_kochi/`
- ❌ No dates: `/free-ewaste-pickup-2025/`
- ❌ No stop words if possible: avoid "the", "and", "in"
- ❌ No duplicate keywords: `/ewaste-ewaste-pickup-kochi/`
