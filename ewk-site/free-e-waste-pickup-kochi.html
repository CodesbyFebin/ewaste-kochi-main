Why is e-waste pickup completely free?

Many people are surprised to learn that professional, certified e-waste pickup in Kochi can genuinely be free. Here is why: The Government of India's Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) system requires electronics manufacturers to fund proper e-waste collection and recycling. As a CPCB-authorized EPR partner, Ewaste Kochi receives EPR credits and funding that offset our collection costs entirely. Additionally, electronic waste contains valuable materials — copper, aluminium, gold, silver, and rare earth elements — whose recovery value further subsidizes the collection operation. This sustainable business model means there is absolutely no reason to charge you. Our revenue comes from responsible recycling and material recovery, not from pickup fees. The free service also ensures that e-waste is properly channeled through authorized recyclers rather than being dumped illegally or handed to unauthorized scrap dealers who lack the capability to handle hazardous components safely.
