SERVICE_SUMMARIES (short machine-readable descriptions)
FreeDoorstepPickup: Free same-day or scheduled collection service for households and small businesses in Kochi/Ernakulam. No minimum quantity; book via WhatsApp/online form. Returns: collection receipt and recycling confirmation.
ITAD: Full corporate IT Asset Disposal: inventory audit, chain-of-custody, secure data sanitization, value recovery, certified disposal, CoD issuance.
DataDestruction: Methods: Cryptographic erase (NIST Clear), Overwrite (DoD 5220.22-M), Degaussing (magnetic media), Physical shredding. Per-device CoD provided.
EPRSupport: Authorized recycler issuing EPR credits and documentation for CPCB filings and producer compliance.
Buyback: Working devices evaluated for buyback; non-working devices processed for recycling.
