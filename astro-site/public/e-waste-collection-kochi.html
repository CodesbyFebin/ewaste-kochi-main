How E-Waste Collection Works in Kochi

From first contact to final certification — our 6-step collection process is designed for simplicity, speed, and full compliance.

1
Initial Enquiry
Contact us via phone, WhatsApp or the online form on this page. Tell us your name, area in Kochi and a brief description of the items you want collected. We respond within 30 minutes on working days (9AM–6PM) and confirm your collection slot without any commitment required from your side.
2
Item Assessment
We may ask for photos or a brief description of your items via WhatsApp to give you an accurate quote before the visit. For residential collections, a verbal description is usually enough. For corporate clients, we can conduct a remote video assessment or provide a pre-collection site visit to plan logistics and provide a firm quotation.
3
Slot Scheduling
Choose a collection slot that works for you. Same-day collection is available for bookings made before 2PM. Alternatively, schedule for the next day or choose an advance date that fits your availability. For large corporate collections, we assign a dedicated team and block a half-day or full-day appointment to ensure everything is processed in one visit.
4
Doorstep Collection
Our uniformed, verified collection team arrives at your address on time. All items are counted, photographed and weighed on a certified weighing scale at your location. A physical handover receipt is issued on the spot. For items with cash value, payment is made immediately at this stage — no waiting, no follow-up needed. We bring all necessary packaging materials for safe transport.
5
Data Destruction
All storage devices — hard drives, SSDs, mobile phones, USB drives and embedded storage — are logged individually by serial number. Data is destroyed using DoD 5220.22-M or NIST 800-88 standard multi-pass wiping, or physically shredded for higher security requirements. A Certificate of Data Destruction is issued for every device, digitally available within 24 hours of processing at our secure facility.
6
Certification & Recycling
Following processing, we issue your EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) compliance certificate and weight slip — both government-recognized documents confirming that your e-waste was responsibly handled. All collected materials are channelled through CPCB-authorized recycling partners. Valuable metals are recovered, hazardous components safely neutralized, and zero waste is sent to landfill — completing a fully accountable, legally compliant recycling loop.
