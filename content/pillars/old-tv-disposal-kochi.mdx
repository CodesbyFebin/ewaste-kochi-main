# Old TV Disposal in Kochi — Safe, Free & Responsible

Got a bulky CRT TV or a broken flat screen taking up space? We collect old televisions of all types from homes across Kochi with free pickup and zero hassle.

## Why Proper TV Disposal Matters

Old televisions — especially CRT models — contain hazardous materials including lead, mercury, and cadmium. Dumping them in regular garbage or by the roadside:

- Contaminates soil and groundwater in your neighbourhood.
- Is illegal under India's E-Waste Management Rules (2022).
- Exposes waste pickers to toxic chemicals without protection.

Handing your TV to a certified recycler like us ensures safe dismantling and recovery of materials without any environmental harm.

## CRT TV Disposal — Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to pay to dispose of an old CRT TV?**
No. We remove CRT televisions at no cost to you. CRTs contain hazardous lead glass that requires special handling, so there is no scrap payout — but disposal is completely free.

**Can you carry a CRT TV down from upstairs?**
Yes — our team handles all loading and heavy lifting. Just let us know in advance if it's on an upper floor.

**Will you accept a TV without a remote or stand?**
Yes. Remotes and stands are not required.

**Is there a minimum or maximum size?**
We accept all sizes, from small 14-inch CRTs to large 65-inch smart TVs.
