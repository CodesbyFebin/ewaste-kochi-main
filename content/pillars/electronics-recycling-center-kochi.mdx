## Why You Should Recycle Electronics Properly

India's E-Waste Management Rules (2022) classify discarded electronics as hazardous waste. When old phones, laptops, and appliances end up in landfills:

- **Lead and mercury** leach into groundwater.
- **Toxic fumes** are released when e-waste is informally burned.
- **Recoverable materials** — copper, gold, and rare metals — are lost forever.

Certified recycling recovers these materials safely, reducing the need to mine new resources.
